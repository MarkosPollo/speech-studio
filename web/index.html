    A little guide to your practice
    
          
        
    
        
      
          
    1. 
            Enable your camera and microphone. Choose the mode
            you need, then allow your browser’s permission prompt.
          
      2. 
          
    2. 
            Load your slides. Choose one PDF or several PNG,
            JPG or WebP images. Export PowerPoint or Keynote decks as PDF first.
            Use Previous / Next or the arrow keys; choose Slides focus for a
            larger slide. Slides stay in this tab and need to be loaded again
            after a refresh.
          
      3. 
          
    3. 
            Choose script or cue cards. Adjust the text size.
            Use Start scroll for a hands-free script, or move through the six
            cue cards.
          
      4. 
          
    4. 
            Press Record take. A three-second countdown gives
            you time to settle. Pause stops both recording and the timer; Stop
            opens your take for review.
          
      5. 
          
    5. 
            Watch, listen, download. Look for clear pauses, a
            relaxed pace and moments you can look up. Download before closing
            this tab or recording a new take.

    Keyboard shortcuts
    
          
    
            Space starts or pauses script scrolling. Left and right arrows
            change slides when slides are visible, or cue cards otherwise. Shift
            + left/right always changes cue cards in Cue cards view. Shortcuts
            stay out of your way while you’re typing or using a control.

    Your target and Learn mode
    
          
    
            Edit Target minutes to suit your talk. Closing reminders follow that
            target; pauses do not count. Learn offers section practice, fewer
            prompts and self-checks without microphone permission. Optional
            local transcription requires an explicit model download and never
            uploads your speech.
          
    
          
    Private, on your device
    
          
    
            Your camera and microphone are used only in this browser. Recordings
            and loaded slides are never uploaded. Slides are a reference while
            you rehearse and are not included in the saved camera/microphone
            recording. Recordings are not automatically saved. Script edits stay
            in this browser; clearing site data removes those edits.
          
    
          
    If camera access is blocked
    
          
    
            Open this site in a full browser tab using HTTPS. Check the
            browser’s camera and microphone settings and close other apps using
            your camera. You can choose camera only or microphone only and try
            again.
