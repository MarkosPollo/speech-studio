Slide descriptions

  1. What does it take to use something again? Three boxes connect a clear question, a simple model and evidence. The theme is reusable hardware and complete-system testing.
  2. Two stages. Different jobs. A launch box branches into first-stage recovery and a second stage continuing with the payload. Recovery is mission-dependent and not guaranteed.
  3. Recovery is part of a larger cycle. Four boxes form a loop: fly, recover, inspect, prepare. This is a conceptual teaching model, not an internal SpaceX checklist.
  4. Demo-2: test the whole journey. A sequence shows launch, in-orbit, docking and landing, the scope of the crew transportation test. The slide identifies May 30, 2020, astronauts Robert Behnken and Douglas Hurley, and Crew Dragon.
  5. Explain the relationship, not every detail. Three communication lessons: state the question, show the handoff, and separate facts from interpretation.
  6. Your next explanation. Three boxes invite you to describe parts, handoffs and evidence in a system from your own work. The slide names the two NASA sources below.

Primary sources

  • NASA Launch Services Program: Falcon 9 supports the two-stage and reuse description.
  • NASA Demo-2 launch release, May 30, 2020 supports the date, crew, vehicle and end-to-end test scope.
