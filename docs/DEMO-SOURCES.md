# Independent SpaceX educational demo

The script, cue cards and six sample slides are original teaching content. They are not an official SpaceX presentation and imply no affiliation or endorsement by SpaceX or NASA. No company logos, official slides or third-party photographs are included. Diagrams are conceptual, not to scale, and are not operational procedures.

The recovery/preparation cycle and communication lessons are the author's conceptual models and interpretation. They do not describe an internal company checklist. The demo makes no current launch-count, valuation, launch-schedule, development-status or safety-guarantee claims. Reuse is not guaranteed on every mission. The ten-minute checkpoints are rehearsal prompts, not measured flight timings or a promised reading duration.

The slide text and descriptions are available at `web/demo/sources.html`. The demo PDF contains neutral title and author metadata. Source pages are linked rather than copied.
