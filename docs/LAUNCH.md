## Short post

Meet Speech Studio: a free-to-use browser rehearsal room with slides, a teleprompter, editable cue cards and local camera or audio recording. No account required. Try the independent SpaceX educational demo, then bring your own talk: https://markospollo.github.io/speech-studio/

## Longer introduction

Practice your next presentation with your slides, script and camera in one workspace. Speech Studio lets you adjust text size and autoscroll, switch to cue cards, and record a take with a ten-minute rehearsal timer. Review and download before closing the tab.

Selected slides and recordings stay in your browser; there is no app upload or transcription service. The included SpaceX example uses original diagrams and linked primary sources, with no official affiliation. Bring your own PDF slides and make the words your own.

Try it: https://markospollo.github.io/speech-studio/

Source and project notes: https://github.com/MarkosPollo/speech-studio

## Release summary

Initial public version: PDF and image slides, slide navigation, three workspace layouts, editable script and cue cards, autoscroll, text sizing, camera/audio modes, countdown, pause/resume, playback and downloads. Browser permissions and recording formats vary; see the browser support notes before relying on a recording for an important event.
